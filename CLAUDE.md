## Rule summary (by section)
- **1 Umpire**: min 2 umpires. Judgement calls final; rule-interpretation inquiry only by head coach → plate umpire, who consults base umpire. Umpires consult, never overrule each other. Forced play: ball must BEAT runner + fielder in control. Disrepute: warning (discretionary) then ejection; refusal to leave = forfeit 9-0; warnings recorded on scorecard.
- **2 Safety**: enclosed shoes, no metal cleats/screw studs. Helmets: batters, runners, catchers; pitcher helmet + facemask + chest plate. No jewellery (medical alert taped).
- **3 Tee**: on home plate, stem over point. Only umpire handles tee; plate ump picks up after each hit, holds until Time.
- **4 Scorers**: sit behind home outside dead ball line; notify umpire of batting out of order + 9th batter done.
- **5 Coaches**: batting team coaches only in 1st/3rd boxes or bench; must stay in box lines (≥1 warning before removal). Coach out of box interferes → lead runner out. Fielding coaches on bench, except 9s & under one coach behind outfield; live ball touches them or they assist fielder → home run to batter. Coach in box intentionally handles live ball → Time, umps decide. Coach physically assisting runner → runner out (congrats contact OK, feet in box).
- **6 Injuries**: Time, umps decide play outcome. Incapacitated player removed w/o penalty, can't re-enter. Non-incapacitated may leave for first aid, return same batting spot. Blood → must leave, clean, cover. Sub runner (6.3): pre-injured batter (tell ump before batting, sub at 1st after Time) or runner injured safe on base; sub = last batter who scored or was put out.
- **7 Specs**: baselines 18.29m; dead ball line 6.10m out; pitcher plate 14.02m. Balls: Incrediball U7; teeball U7/9/10; baseball U11-13. Bat max 86.4cm/5.7cm, certified stamp (single-piece wood/foam exempt). Glove ≤32.5x20cm.
- **8 Conduct**: as 1 (warning → removal → forfeit 9-0).
- **9 Game**: 9 innings or 1hr. No innings starts within 5 min of finish time; started innings completed (equal digs). Innings = 3 outs or 9 batters. Indicating 9th batter: batting team → batter out + side away; fielding team → batter home run (still touch bases).
- **10 Play Ball**: "Batter Up" then "Play Ball" each batter and after strike/foul/dead ball/Time; not until runners on bases + fielders settled.
- **11 Time**: called when ball held by infield player, no play being made, runners within ~3 paces of base. One runner per base.
- **12 Catch**: secure in hand/glove, in flight, fair or foul; not using uniform deliberately. Drop while making second play still catch.
- **13 Foul/Strikes**: foul = first lands foul/dead ball territory or touches fielder/ump over foul. Judge by where ball would've landed. Can't be foul until touches ground. Foul → Time, runners return, strike. Strikes: foul; full swing misses; more tee than ball (not if reaches mid-outfield); not full forceful swing; back foot movement; out of box; stepping on plate while hitting; ball first lands on home plate. 3 strikes out.
- **14 Fair**: first lands fair / on line / hits ump or fielder over fair; settles in field after landing fair; caught anywhere. Caught ball live, runners advance at own risk.
- **15 Dead ball**: ball over dead ball line; into occupied warm-up circle; injury; danger; fielder deliberately puts ball over line. Batted ball off fielder (fair) over line → runners get base they were going to. Thrown ball over → 17.3. Batted ball over line straight off bat → dead, strike, bat again. Others: umps consult on outcome.
- **16 Batting**: up to 12 listed, 9 bat per innings, next innings starts with next listed. 9 batted <3 outs → Side Away. <7 players forfeit 9-0 (opposition may lend 1-2). 7-8 players → spots 8/9 automatic outs. Batting order fixed once batted. Out of order (16.3): Time, runners back, proper batter inherits strikes; improper batter legal once Play Ball called for next batter. Back foot reposition after Play Ball → foul + strike (pivot/drag after contact OK). Hit before Play Ball → dead, no count. Foot completely outside box at contact → strike, dead. Bat hits ball 2nd time in fair → out. Thrown bat (any direction incl ground) → out, dead, runners return. Bat not dropped by halfway to 1st → out, dead, runners return (halfway = judgement).
- **17 Running**: leaving diamond = done for that innings. Runner removes helmet → returned to last base crossed (if not put out). Overthrow over dead ball line → next base from where they were. Base not legally crossed until touched or Play Ball on next batter. Leaving base before hit → dead, all return, warning; repeat same runner same innings → out; no catch/count. Batter runs 2nd half to 1st outside diamond else out if play made. Overrun 1st OK if return immediately; out if intent toward 2nd + tagged. Missed base: appeal holding ball on base; only if ump saw it + correct runner identified first appeal; retouch in reverse order; can't return to occupied base. Tag = ball or hand/glove holding ball; base tag = any body part w/ control; must keep control. Force = no empty base behind. Leave before catch + not retouch before tag → out. Passing runner ahead → out. No runs on 3rd out by batter-runner before 1st or force play; otherwise runs before 3rd out count. Incitement running/barging → nullify advantage, warn; severe → out/eject coach (≥1 warning). Dislodged base: no play on runner who reached it safely; following runners use marked spot; leaving it loses exemption. Deviating from line to avoid tag → out (slide near base OK; may turn back shortest line if not forced).
- **18 Interference**: runner interferes w/ fielder on batted ball → out, dead, no advance unless forced. On thrown ball only if intentional, same penalty. Out runner interferes → umps decide.
- **19 Fielding**: 9 fielders. No player sits 2 consecutive fielding innings unless requested (warn coach, then remove coach). From Play Ball to swing: orthodox positions, set (outfielders not running), pitcher on plate + only fielder inside diamond, catcher behind back line of box opposite batter (arm only forward, not into box), only catcher in foul territory, limited movement, pitcher/catcher stationary. Violation gaining advantage → dead ball, runners return, warn. Pitcher leaves plate before hit → dead, return, warn; 2nd+ same innings → runners advance 1 base. Fielder removes helmet while live: interference by it → all runners +1, no outs. Removed uniform/equipment used to stop ball → home run.
- **20 Obstruction**: fielder w/o ball not fielding impedes runner → penalty nullifying it; runners advance ≥1 base beyond where going.
- **21 Substitution**: listed players swap fielders anytime after Time; can't circumvent 19.2. Official sub: replaced player out of game, takes batting spot; only if team has 12 listed; subs listed as reserves.
- **22 Ages**: U7, U9, U10, U11, U12, U13. Must be under age as at 30 June of year season finishes.

## Rulings log
Record situational questions + answers here for future reference.

### 1. Runner leaves 1st on hit, ball caught, runner doesn't return
Q: Runner on 1st runs on hit, ball caught (batter out), runner doesn't return, called out. Told runner can only run once ball caught — correct?
A: Explanation wrong. Leaving on the hit is legal (17.4 only bans leaving before hit). But 17.10: runner who left before catch is out if they fail to re-touch base before they or that base is tagged by fielder w/ ball. Not automatic — needs tag of runner or 1st base. Must retouch in reverse order (17.7), e.g. back via 2nd if passed it. After retouching may advance at own risk (17.11, 14 note iii).
Follow-up: applies to any catch incl deep outfield. Only if runner left before catch. Not "always": out only if tagged (runner or base) before retouch — no tag = no out, even if runner never returns. Runner may run back, retouch, then advance at own risk (tag up). Manual 17.10 confirms: fielder w/ control of caught ball just touches/runs across base runner left; no verbal appeal needed; ball beats runner back = Out, let play flow. Manual 17.11: deep outfield catches = typical tag-up spot.
Follow-up 2: runner stays on 2nd, no tag, Time called → runner safe, stays on 2nd. Tags only while ball live (17.8); Time = dead (11). Grey area NOT resolved by manual: manual 17.11 calls it being "put out on appeal (see 17.10)", and missed-base appeals (17.7) allowed until Play Ball on next batter — so fielding side could argue they can still touch 1st after Time. Manual 17.11 also: don't call Time on catch unless ball in infield + all runners near their base. Lean: allow touch of 1st before next Play Ball (appeal-style); umpire discretion.
Follow-up 3: runner ran on to 3rd/home. Only the base they left (1st) or the runner counts (17.10 "that base"); tagging 2nd/3rd/home = nothing. Runner must retouch reverse order 3rd→2nd→1st (17.7). If reached home then 1st tagged → out, run cancelled. Grey area: tagging runner while standing on 3rd — 17.10 no off-base qualifier but 17.8 says "whilst off base"; lean out (3rd not their legal base). Manual doesn't address.

### 2. Does ball need to be inside diamond for Time?
A: No. Rule 11: "held by any player in an infield position (with no play being made) and all runners are in close proximity to a base". Says infield position, not inside diamond. Infield positions (1B/2B/SS/3B) mostly sit outside diamond (19.6), so "inside diamond" can't be requirement. "Any player" → outfielder running into infield area should count (umpire judgement). Coach's "throw it in" = good practice (faster, removes doubt), not a rule.
Manual confirms (p23): infield position = "area normally occupied by players other than the orthodox left, centre and right field", relative to where infielders are fielding that game (U7 closer than U13). Ball must be securely held. Proximity = ~3 paces either side of base, scaled to age. Runner halfway up line → shouldn't call Time.

### 3. Fielders without ball blocking bases/baselines (esp home)
A: Obstruction (20) if fielder not holding ball AND not in act of fielding AND impedes runner. Contact not required; runner slowing/deviating from fear = impeded (judgement). Manual (p48) confirms: no contact needed; example = baseman straddling base w/o ball. Intent: stop fielders occupying bases before entitled — "only entitled to do so when they have control of the ball". Award where runner would have ended (can be >1 extra base; don't Time early and give just 1). Umpire should tell fielding coach to talk to player. Runners NOT allowed to run through blockers — out for charging. Fielder receiving throw: "act of fielding" (manual 18.1) = must be in that spot to make the play / about to catch — narrow. Penalty: umpire nullifies, runner awarded ≥1 base beyond where going. Umpire's call (20 "umpire shall invoke"); judgement final (1.2b). Coach may raise respectfully w/ plate umpire, head coach only. If deliberate/coached: safety (1.1i, 2.3g), discourage exploiting rules (1.2d), disrepute warning → ejection (1.1, 8).

### 4. Fielder stands on 2nd awaiting throw, runner arrives before ball
A: Obstruction if fielder blocks runner's path/base and runner gets there first. Manual p48: fielders "only entitled" to occupy bases w/ control of ball. "Act of fielding" (manual 18.1) narrow: about to catch / no other spot possible — receiving throw can be done beside base, so ball still in flight + runner first ≠ fielding. Ball + runner arrive together = judgement; contact during genuine catch attempt usually no penalty either way (18.2 needs intent). Manual p42 (17.14): coaches should teach fielders to stand off running line, tag from side; runner must not charge (out). Correct technique: stand beside base, foot on edge (force), take throw, tag base/runner from side. Penalty: runner ≥1 extra base beyond 2nd (i.e. 3rd) or further if would have gone further. Also rule 1.2a: ball must BEAT runner on force.

### 5. 1st baseman stretched out, only edge of foot on base, catches before runner
A: Legal force out. 17.8 note ii: tagging base = touching base w/ ANY part of body whilst in control of ball. Distance of rest of body irrelevant. Conditions: foot touching base at moment of catch (not lifted/swept), ball in glove/hand (manual p40: ball trapped on ground under glove ≠ control), retains control after (note iii), ball beats runner (1.2a). Batter-runner always forced at 1st (17.9). Actually good technique (off running line, cf. ruling 4). Only issue: stretched leg across runner's path before ball arrives could be obstruction (20).

### 6. Ball first lands fair (halfway home-3rd), rolls foul before 3rd — fair or foul?
A: Fair. Tee-ball decides on FIRST LANDING only (13.1a, 14a, 14c) — NOT baseball rule (where foul before passing 1st/3rd = foul). 14(c): settles anywhere on playing field having first landed fair = fair. Manual p27 (14c): "a ball that first lands fair then rolls and settles in foul territory is still a fair ball." Landing on line = fair (14b). Coach's view = baseball rule, not in TBAWA rules. Manual: umps only call FOUL, never signal fair; may quietly hint very young players to run.

### 7. Forced runner 1st→2nd: tag runner OR touch 2nd?
A: Either one, not both. 17.8(a): forced play → base tagged before runner arrives OR runner tagged before reaching base. Tag = ball or hand/glove holding ball, keep control after (17.8 notes i, iii). Catch: force removed once batter-runner put out before 1st (batter tagged or 1st touched) → then touching 2nd no longer counts, must tag runner. CONFLICT: manual 17.9 (p40) also says "once the batter reaches first the force no longer stands" — rulebook 17.9 wording (no empty base behind runner) implies force continues; baseball too. Unresolved; ask Chief Umpire/club. Tagging the runner always works either way. Tag of forced runner = still force play → if 3rd out, no runs count (17.13b), even runner from 2nd crossing home first.

### 8. Runs scored before 3rd out — count?
A: Depends on type of 3rd out (17.13). NO runs if 3rd out = (a) batter-runner before touching 1st, or (b) any force play (tag of forced runner or base) — even if runner crossed home earlier. Otherwise runs crossed before 3rd out COUNT (e.g. 3rd out = tag on non-forced runner, runner tagged off 1st after overrun). Manual p41-42 scenarios: 1st+3rd, run crosses, runner to 2nd forced out = no run. 2nd+3rd, 2nd out at 3rd, run crosses, batter out at 1st = no run. 2nd+3rd, batter out at 1st (2nd out), run crosses, runner from 2nd tagged at 3rd (not forced, 1st empty after batter out) = run counts. Timing matters: runner must cross home BEFORE the 3rd out.

### 9. When is runner 1st→2nd NOT forced?
A: Force removed when batter-runner is out: (1) batted ball caught (12) — runner must retouch 1st if left before catch (17.10), then MAY advance at own risk (17.11), not obliged; (2) batter-runner tagged before 1st or 1st touched first (manual 17.9 p40). After force removed: touching 2nd = nothing; must tag runner off base (17.8b). If ball caught + runner off 1st: touching 1st also gets them (17.10). Manual also claims force ends once batter reaches 1st — conflicts w/ rulebook 17.9, see ruling 7. Batter out via thrown bat / bat not dropped (16.8, 16.9) = dead ball, runners return, not a force question.

### 10. Too much tee (13.2c), but ball caught in the air (pitcher/catcher)
A: Out. 13.1 note iii: ball can't be declared foul until it touches ground; 14(d): caught anywhere = fair. Manual p60 "Tee Foul" procedure: pick up tee, WAIT until ball hits ground, then call "Time, Foul Ball". Manual p26 (not full swing): "Should the ball be caught the batter is out"; same wait-for-catch logic for back foot (16.4) + out of box (16.6). Which fielder catches doesn't matter; must be a legal catch (12: secure, in flight). Exception: hit before Play Ball (16.5) or runner left early (17.4) = dead ball, catch doesn't count. Ball live after catch, runners may advance at own risk (17.11).

### 11. 3rd out at 3rd base, 2 runners ahead already crossed home — count?
A: Depends if out at 3rd was force (17.13b). Runner forced to 3rd only if bases behind full: e.g. bases loaded, runner from 2nd forced → base tag or tag = force out → NO runs. Runner from 1st going to 3rd (only forced to 2nd) or runner from 2nd w/ 1st empty → not forced, must be tagged → runs crossed before tag COUNT. Ruling-7 conflict applies: per manual 17.9, once batter reaches 1st force ends → bases-loaded case would become tag play + runs count; rulebook reading = still force. Timing: run must cross home before tag (umpire watch both).

### 12. Non-forced runner stranded between 2nd-3rd (3rd occupied), fielder w/ ball between runner and 2nd, no tag attempt
User called Time, runner safe, returned to 2nd.
A: Not tagged = not out — correct. 3rd occupied so can't go there (11 one runner per base; 17.7). Fielder holds ball → NOT obstruction (20 needs fielder w/o ball). Runner can't charge (manual p42) or deviate from line to avoid tag (17.16; may turn back on shortest line). Time: rule 11 needs ALL runners within ~3 paces of base. If runner within ~3 paces of 2nd → Time + return to 2nd = correct. If midway → manual p23: "YOU SHOULD NOT HAVE CALLED TIME"; keep ball live — fielding side must tag or runner gets back; manual gives no rule for where to send runner if Time called early. Returning to 2nd (last base held) = reasonable fix. Fielding side choosing not to tag doesn't make play dead.

### 13. Same fielder in same position consecutive innings — rule?
A: Wembley: YES. WTBA By-Law 12.3 (p6): teams must rotate fielding positions in regular season; player can't play same position 2 consecutive fielding innings. TBAWA rules themselves have no rotation rule; only 19.2: no listed player sits out 2 consecutive fielding innings (unless at their request to umpire); warn coach, 2nd time coach removed (repeated in By-Law 12.4). Manual 19.2 intent: participation / "fair go". 21: subs can't circumvent 19.2. By-laws give no penalty for rotation breach (12.4 penalty is for sitting out). (Initially answered "no" before by-laws were added.)
